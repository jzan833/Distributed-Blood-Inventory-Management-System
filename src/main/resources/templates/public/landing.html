Blood Knowledge

Understanding Blood Donation

Knowledge empowers action. Learn the basics of blood compatibility, donation safety, and how you can help.

Blood Types Matter

There are 8 main blood types. O-negative is the universal donor, while AB-positive is the universal recipient.

90-Day Rule

Whole blood donors must wait 90 days between donations. This allows your body to replenish iron and red cells safely.

Safe Process

Every donation is screened, tested, and stored under strict medical protocols. Donating blood is safe for healthy adults.

Who Can Donate

Most people between 18-65 years, weighing over 50kg, and in good health can donate. Check your eligibility today.
